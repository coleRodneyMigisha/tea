Small teams need fewer documents, not better ones

Most small teams write more documents than anyone reads. This post shows how to tell which documents earn their keep, and how to drop the rest without losing what matters.

Why most documents go unread

A document is useful only when someone opens it at the moment they need it. Teams of five rarely have that moment, because the answer is usually one message away.

Three questions before you write anything

Ask who will read it, when, and what they will do differently. If you cannot name a reader, the page is a diary entry.
